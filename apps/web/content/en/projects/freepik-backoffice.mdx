## Operational Context

Behind a global catalog of tens of millions of creative assets lies a massive production and review engine: thousands of independent contributors uploading illustrations, vectors, photography, PSD files, 3D models, and AI-generated assets daily, alongside internal teams focused on curation and in-house asset generation.

The Freepik and Flaticon content backoffice is the internal enterprise suite powering this digital supply chain at scale.

## High-Volume Moderation, Tagging & Ingestion

The core backoffice processes millions of incoming submissions through high-throughput tools:

- **Cataloging & Tagging**: Multi-language metadata tagging and category indexing designed to maximize search discoverability across Freepik's engine.
- **Quality & Copyright Moderation**: Rapid review interfaces and intellectual property verification systems ensuring platform guideline compliance.
- **System Modernization**: Progressive migration from legacy administrative portals toward a modern stack built on Laravel and Vue 3 / TypeScript, transitioning critical operational flows with zero downtime.

## Content Production Workflows

Internal creative production is structured as a state-driven workflow:

- Task dispatching tailored to contributor expertise and capacity.
- Review cycles, version control, and final publishing with format-specific validation rules.
- Real-time performance dashboards allowing coordinators to track production velocity and balance workloads.

## Flaticon Icon Packs & Contributor Tax Portal

- **Icon Family Composition**: Specialized tooling for organizing, versioning, and publishing consistent icon packs across Flaticon.
- **Contributor Tax & Payout Portal**: Secure interface for international tax documentation and financial verification, implementing upfront validations that prevent accounting rejections.
- **Productivity Chrome Extensions**: Custom browser extensions automating repetitive moderation actions and keyboard shortcuts, saving operational teams thousands of manual interactions daily.
