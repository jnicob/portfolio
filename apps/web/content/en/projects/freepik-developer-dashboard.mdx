## The product

Building on the Freepik/Magnific API takes more than endpoints: developers
need to understand what they consume, what remains, what is failing and
how to manage their credentials. The developer dashboard provides that
management and analytics layer—the operational face of the platform for
B2B customers and individual developers—across the developer hub and the
account profile area.

## Usage and budget

- Consumption charts by period make each integration's activity and
  per-service detail visible at a glance.
- Rate limits are visible in context: each plan has its own limits, and
  the dashboard shows where customers stand before a limit becomes an error.
- The credit budget compares accumulated usage with the contracted amount,
  helping customers expand capacity before running out.

## API keys and teams

- Create, rotate and revoke API keys without contacting support.
- Enterprise member rules let large teams control who can create keys and
  spend credits, with the organisation—not the individual—as the billing unit.

## B2B: credits and plans

The same dashboard serves an individual developer on an entry plan and an
organisation with an enterprise agreement. Consumption, limits and billing
adapt to the contracted plan. Deciding what appears, what can be expanded
and where limits apply is part of the product rather than an added sales layer.

## One connected platform

The dashboard is the last mile of the [API platform](./freepik-api-platform):
what developers subscribe to and consume there is what the public pages explain.
